# KidBase — Problem Discovery

## Current Process

### Enrollment

A new child is enrolled through a physical contract and required licensing forms. Parents manually complete and sign the paperwork, which is then collected by the daycare.

### Attendance

Daily attendance is recorded manually on a paper list.

### Child Information

Child information is stored and maintained through physical documents and paper records.

### Parent/Guardian Information

Parent and guardian information is collected and maintained through physical documents and paper records.

### Documents

After parents complete and sign the required forms, the documents are stored individually in a designated location.

### Payments

Tuition payments are primarily received through Zelle. Payments are not currently tracked in a centralized system; the daycare mainly receives the payment and verifies it.

### Daily Operations

Menus and activities are managed manually. A menu table is used for meals, while activities are generally repeated and are not organized through a structured schedule or system.

### Infant Nap Monitoring

For babies, staff manually record updates approximately every 15 minutes on paper. During these intervals, staff check the baby's position, activity, and other relevant observations and document the information manually.

## Current Problems

- **Manual paperwork:** Many processes depend on physical forms and documents.
- **Paper-based attendance:** Recording and reviewing attendance manually can be time-consuming and prone to errors.
- **Scattered information:** Child and parent information is stored in physical documents, making information harder to find and update.
- **Document organization:** Managing individual physical documents can make it difficult to quickly locate specific records.
- **Limited payment tracking:** Payments received through Zelle are not maintained in a centralized payment history.
- **Repetitive administrative work:** Staff and owners repeatedly perform tasks that could potentially be standardized or automated.
- **Limited operational organization:** Menus and activities are managed manually without a centralized schedule or structured system.
- **Manual infant monitoring records:** Staff must repeatedly check and manually document infant observations every 15 minutes, creating a frequent and time-consuming task.
- **Difficulty accessing historical information:** Reviewing previous attendance, payments, documents, or other records can require manually searching through physical records.
