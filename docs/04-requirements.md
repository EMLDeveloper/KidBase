# KidBase — Requirements

## Child Management

- The system shall allow authorized staff to create a child profile.
- The system shall allow authorized staff to update child information.
- The system shall allow authorized staff to view child information.

## Parent / Guardian Management

- The system shall allow staff to create parent/guardian profiles.
- The system shall allow staff to associate parents/guardians with children.
- The system shall allow staff to update parent/guardian information.

## Enrollment

- The system shall allow authorized users to create an enrollment record.
- The system shall store enrollment information digitally.
- The system shall track the child's enrollment status.

## Attendance

- The system shall allow staff to record a child's check-in time.
- The system shall allow staff to record a child's check-out time.
- The system shall allow authorized users to view attendance history.

## Document Management

- The system shall allow authorized users to upload and store required documents.
- The system shall associate documents with the appropriate child.
- The system shall allow authorized users to view and manage documents.

## Payments

- The system shall allow authorized users to record tuition payments.
- The system shall maintain a payment history for each child.
- The system shall allow authorized users to view outstanding balances.

## Menus & Activities

- The system shall allow staff to create and manage meal menus.
- The system shall allow staff to record daily activities.
- The system shall allow authorized users to view previous menus and activities.

## Infant Monitoring

- The system shall allow staff to record infant observations.
- The system shall record the time of each observation.
- The system shall allow staff to record the baby's position and relevant observations.
- The system shall maintain a history of infant monitoring records.
