# KidBase — Product Vision

## What is KidBase?

KidBase is a preschool and daycare management system dedicated to automating the daily, repetitive, and time-consuming tasks that burden childcare businesses — such as paperwork, attendance tracking, and document management — making business operations smoother, faster, and more efficient.

## Who is it for?

- **Owners / Administrators** — Manage the daycare's operations, staff, children, enrollment, payments, and records.
- **Staff / Assistants** — Manage daily activities such as attendance, child information, meals, and other daycare tasks.
- **Parents / Guardians** — Access relevant information about their children, enrollment, attendance, updates, and other records.
- **Children** — Benefit indirectly from a more organized and efficient daycare environment.

## What problem does it solve?

KidBase addresses repetitive and time-consuming tasks involved in the daily operation of a daycare or preschool, such as:

- Attendance tracking
- Document management
- Storing and updating child information
- Managing parent/guardian information
- Enrollment management
- Daily updates
- Menu management
- Maintaining business records
- Mail and messaging
- Nap time tracking

## What is the main goal?

The main goal of KidBase is to streamline and automate the operational and administrative processes of preschools and daycares, reducing manual workload, minimizing human error, and allowing staff and owners to focus on what matters most: the children.
