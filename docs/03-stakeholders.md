# KidBase — Stakeholders

## 1. Owner / Administrator

- Full access to the system
- Manages enrollment, billing, staff, and reports
- Makes all business decisions

## 2. Staff / Teacher Assistants

- Check children in and out
- View child profiles and medical notes
- Manage daily activities

## 3. Parents / Guardians

- Receive notifications about their child
- View and pay invoices
- Access their child's profile and updates

## 4. Children

- Core subject of the system
- All data revolves around them
- Do not interact with the system directly
